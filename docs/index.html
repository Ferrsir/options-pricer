Where the P&L comes from

Edge at entry = model value − your premium (from your side). Then the Greeks explain the move: Δ·ΔS, ½Γ·ΔS², vega·Δσ, Θ·Δt. “Other” is the higher-order remainder.

P&L heat map

Green = profit, red = loss, per share, valued with Black-Scholes. The dotted vertical line is where you are now.

Convergence to Black-Scholes-Merton

European tree error shrinks roughly like 1/steps (with a saw-tooth from how the strike sits on the lattice). The American curve sits above it by the early-exercise premium.

Early-exercise boundary

Pipeline: log-moneyness k = ln(K/S) → OTM quotes → Black-Scholes implied vols → Savitzky-Golay smoothing per expiry → two-stage PCHIP interpolation (strike, then total variance in time). The red dashed line marks at-the-money.
